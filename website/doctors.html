Working together

A directory is useful. A connected team is better.

The prototype groups roles by what people need, not by internal hospital structure. This keeps specialist, nursing and allied-health support visible within the same journey.

  • Plain-language roles and responsibilities
  • Consistent profile layout for faster scanning
  • Inclusive names and imagery
  • No real qualifications or endorsements implied

Team values

How HavenCare communicates care.

These content principles shape the interface as much as the visual design.

Listen first

Respect different goals, access needs, languages and levels of health literacy.

Explain clearly

Use concise language, descriptive links and predictable page structures.

Protect trust

State prototype limitations and avoid collecting or implying real patient records.
