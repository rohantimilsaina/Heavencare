Admission journey

Four steps, clearly explained.

A progressive disclosure pattern keeps each answer available without turning the page into a wall of text.

1. Before your visit

For a real appointment, confirm the date, arrival time, location and preparation instructions with the health provider. Ask early about access or language support.

2. When you arrive

Follow current facility signage or ask a staffed information point. Allow extra time for registration and bring only information requested by the real provider.

3. During your appointment

Ask questions in plain language, request clarification and take notes if helpful. A support person may be appropriate when permitted by the service.

4. Before you leave

Confirm the next step, who to contact with questions and whether any written instructions are available in an accessible format.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

Is HavenCare a real hospital?

No. HavenCare is a brand created for a static website assignment. No services, clinicians, hours or contact details are real.

Does the appointment form send my information?

No. JavaScript validates entries locally, shows a success message and clears the form. Nothing is transmitted or saved.

Can this website give medical advice?

No. The prototype demonstrates interface design only. For medical questions, contact a qualified health professional or a real local health service.

How do I use the website with a keyboard?

Press Tab to move through links and controls, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and Escape to close the mobile menu or image viewer.

Have a general question?

Use the form without sending real personal information.

The demonstration includes HTML5 constraints, field-level JavaScript messages and an accessible success announcement.
