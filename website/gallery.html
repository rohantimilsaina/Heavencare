Healing environment

Accessibility is visible in the space.

The environment uses uncluttered circulation, step-free routes, seated rest points and open sightlines. The same principles carry into the interface through whitespace, contrast and predictable navigation.

  • Wide paths and clear destinations
  • Shaded rest areas and natural light
  • Low-clutter visual composition
  • Responsive, descriptive digital media
