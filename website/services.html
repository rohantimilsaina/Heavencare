Service coordination

One visit, fewer unknowns.

Every service follows a consistent three-stage information pattern, reducing cognitive load for people who may be anxious, tired or unfamiliar with hospital processes.

  • Before: referral and preparation information
  • During: arrival, accessibility and support guidance
  • After: discharge, follow-up and contact details
